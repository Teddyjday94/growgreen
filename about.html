Built on service, responsibility and doing the job right.

Dereck Palermo founded Grow Green Weed Control with a straightforward idea: South Louisiana homeowners deserved reliable lawn care from a local team that would take ownership of the result.

As a professional firefighter, Dereck built the company around the habits his other career demands: show up prepared, pay attention to details, communicate clearly, and be accountable when people count on you.

What began as one local owner serving his neighbors has grown into a team caring for hundreds of lawns across Baton Rouge, Ascension and Livingston.
